HI, I'M JANSEN — BUT MOST PEOPLE CALL ME OCON. 👋

I’m a 24-year-old Junior Web Developer based in Depok, born in Jakarta, with an educational background in Multimedia.

My journey into web development started from a genuine interest in coding. I’m currently building my understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, while continuously learning how websites are designed, structured, and brought to life.

I may have started this journey a little later than I imagined, but now that I finally have the opportunity to pursue it, I want to take it seriously.

My goal is simple but ambitious: to become a great Web Developer and create digital products that can genuinely be used and enjoyed by people everywhere.
